Privacy Policy

Catapult Castle Crashers · Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information Catapult Castle Crashers (the “app”, package com.brandes.catapult) handles. The app is developed by Ran Brandes (“we”). It is also available as a web game in the browser.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any account details. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so your progress is kept between sessions:

  • Level progress: unlocked levels, stars earned, and best scores.
  • Custom levels you create in the level designer.
  • Your sound setting (mute on or off).

This data never leaves your device. In the Android app it is removed when you uninstall the app. In the web version it is kept in your browser’s site storage for this page and can be cleared through your browser settings.

Data we send or share

We do not transmit, sell, rent or share any data with third parties. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting software.

Permissions

The Android app does not request any special device permissions such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage.

Advertising

The app does not show ads and does not use an advertising identifier.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to delete on request.

Security

Because all progress stays on your device, the main protection is your device’s own security. We recommend keeping your operating system and browser up to date.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
